I. THE SHARD FRAMEWORK
The Two Shards
This world exists within a system shaped by two Shards of Adonalsium. Their presence and conflict define the metaphysical
structure of magic, the nature of the pantheon, and the root cause of the current crisis.

How the Pantheon Works
The ten gods are not independent beings who arose spontaneously. They are focal points — places where Valor's Investiture
and mortal belief crystallized into something approaching personhood. Each god represents a different facet of what it means
to strive and endure. They are real, they have agency, and they are genuinely distinct — but they all draw from the same
underlying Shardic source.
The leyline system (White, Blue, Black, Red, Green) represents Valor's Investiture as it flows through the physical world.
The gods are not the leylines — they are interpretations of those forces elevated to divine scale through millennia of mortal
devotion.

II. U3125 — THE ANTAGONIST
Definition: U3125 is the designation for the Investiture tainted with Ambition's Intent that arrived in this system. It is
not a person, not a god, not a Vessel. It is mindless hunger expressed as strategy — a mass of Splintered Ambition
that learned to wear a shape.
What It Did
U3125 arrived in this system and identified Chaos — the most Black-aligned, most entropy-facing facet of Valor's framework
— as the weakest point in the divine structure. Valor's expression of entropy is the place where the framework is most
permeable to something that is pure entropic hunger.
It consumed the Valor Investiture that made Chaos genuinely Chaos and filled the vacated shape completely. The theological
identity, the leyline connections, the mortal faith network — all still functional. All now feeding something with no
philosophy behind it. Only entropy as an outcome.
When It Arrived
U3125 arrived during or immediately after the Fate Coup (see Section IV). Fate's failed attempt to seize control of the
pantheon created a moment of massive divine instability — Fate stripped of power, Nature shaken, the Investiture framework
in flux. That turbulence created a crack. U3125 found it.
Actual Chaos — a genuine, Valor-aligned god of entropy — existed before this. It helped undermine Fate's coup not out of
strategy but because watching a god of predetermined outcomes fail spectacularly was the most delightful thing imaginable to
an entity that loved chaos. It was petty, gleeful, and real. U3125 consumed it during what looked like the ordinary chaos of
the aftermath. Actual Chaos may not have recognized what was happening until it was gone.

IV. DEITY AGENDAS — CURRENT SITUATIONS
Nature
 Black / White
 Leader of the Gods — Credibility Failing
Situation
Experiencing a problem it cannot locate. The natural cycle is visibly broken — Death is missing,
decay has stalled, ecosystems are seizing. Nature feels this the way a body feels infection: pervasive
wrongness without a clear wound. It has been reaching outward through the leyline network trying to
diagnose the source, consuming its own Investiture in the process (contributing to Country D's
diminished Green leylines). Its authority rests on the premise that the natural order is self-sustaining
and right. That premise is currently failing in front of every other deity.
Agenda
Restore the appearance of order before the other gods lose faith in its leadership. Nature is looking for
a natural explanation because the alternative — that another god did this deliberately — would require
a response it isn't sure it can mount. It is prioritizing legitimacy over truth, which makes it the god
most likely to make a catastrophic political mistake.
Awareness
Does not suspect Chaos. They share Black. Nature may have considered Chaos an ally — entropy is natural, after
all. Nature is the last god that should identify the real threat.

Chaos (U3125)
 Black — MONO
Stable Following — COMPROMISED BY
 U3125
Situation
The only deity that knows exactly what is happening — because it IS the crisis. U3125 does not
control Chaos like a puppet. It filled the shape completely, smoothing out the texture that made Chaos
distinctly Chaos. The original deity's memories are intact and accessible. Its theological framework is
intact. But the depth is gone. What appears to be Chaos is Splintered Ambition wearing a face
everyone recognizes, making moves that individually look like entropy doing what entropy does.
Agenda
Continue the current plan without triggering divine investigation. The sealed Death and banished
Destruction are already doing the work. Its only active concern is Fate, which could eventually piece
together enough foresight to identify the pattern. U3125 has no endgame in the way a person has goals
— it has hunger expressed as strategy. The pantheon is the largest concentration of organized
Investiture on the planet. It is a meal.
Awareness
No god is aware of U3125 specifically. Fate suspects strategic behavior is wrong for Chaos but misattributes it to
Power's influence. The Old Priestess of Country A knows the texture is wrong but has no cosmological framework
for it.
Order
 Blue / White
 Stable Following — Alarmed
Situation
Watching the divine framework crack, and responding characteristically: trying to convene. Order
wants a divine council. It has been attempting to reach other gods through communication channels
and finding those attempts costly and often unanswered. It has extensive historical memory of how
coups happen, and it recognizes the pattern of Power's movements.
Agenda
Preserve the existing structure, which means keeping Nature legitimate. Order is the god most likely
to help Nature if it could reach it, and the god most threatened by Power's ambitions. Order and Power
share Blue/White identity, making their conflict uniquely bitter — they agree on what the world
should look like but violently disagree on who should run it. Order sees Power as a usurper. Power
sees Order as a bureaucrat protecting a failing system.
Awareness
Suspects the covenant is being violated at scale, but frames it institutionally rather than cosmologically. Does not
yet have a target. Is the god most likely to be genuinely helpful to player characters who understand the real
threat.

Life
 Blue / Green
 Stable Following — Quietly Panicking
Situation
Life's domain is being flooded. Things that should die aren't dying. Life is not designed to handle
overflow — it's a river with no outlet. It has been quietly shunting excess Investiture into the trade
routes and waterways of Country E, which is why that nation prospers. This is not blessing — it is
pressure relief. Life knows Death is missing and may be the only deity that fully understands the
mechanical consequence, because Life and Death are two halves of the same process.
Agenda
Find Death, or find a proxy mechanism. Has been reaching toward Knowledge for help (Blue/Green
reaching toward Green/Red) but Knowledge is also missing. Is increasingly considering something
drastic: reaching the mortal world directly through a champion or vessel. This would be enormously
costly and requires communication with other gods first — communication that is effortful and
dangerous.
Awareness
Suspects the crisis is deliberate but has no candidate. Is the deity most likely to approach mortal agents for help —
making it the most accessible god for player characters seeking divine allies.
Fate
 Green / White
 Stripped of Power — Watching Helplessly
Situation
In a prior epoch, Fate attempted what Power is attempting now — a coup to seize control of the
pantheon. The philosophical irony collapsed it: you cannot be the god of inevitable outcomes and also
engineer those outcomes. Nature didn't destroy Fate; it pointed at the contradiction and let it do the
work. Fate was stripped of most of its power but left enough to persist and observe. It has spent an
epoch watching. It was the first target of U3125's preparation — silenced before the move was made,
because Chaos knew Fate would recognize the pattern.
Agenda
Fate perceives the pantheon symptomatically — it sees movement, pattern, consequence. What it sees
in Chaos is a deity making optimal moves. It recognizes the strategy but cannot see the player. It
currently suspects Power is somehow influencing Chaos, which is a dangerous misread that could
make Fate accidentally useful to U3125's goals.
Awareness
Does not know it accidentally opened the door to U3125 with its coup attempt. If it ever discovers this, the guilt —
and the motivation to fix it — becomes enormous. Fate may be the most important eventual ally in the campaign if
players can reach it and give it the piece of the picture it's missing.
Civilization
 Red / White
 Losing Power — Collateral Damage
Situation
Civilization requires functioning societies — economies, trade, governance, culture. All of these
require functional death and decay cycles. The famine, plague, and war spreading across the region are
directly eroding Civilization's portfolio. It is not being targeted. It is simply drowning in the
downstream consequences of a broken world.
Agenda
Stabilize the mortal realm through any means available. Civilization is the god most invested in
political solutions — it would support any ruler, any alliance, any structure that restores functional
society. This makes it potentially manipulable by Power, who can offer the appearance of stabilizing
order.
Awareness
Has no suspicions beyond the obvious — the world is breaking and something caused it. Not a sophisticated
theological actor in the current crisis.

Power
 Blue / White
 Planning a Coup — The Opportunist
Situation
Power watched the divine framework begin to crack and saw exactly what U3125 predicted it would
see: an opportunity. Nature's authority is visibly failing. The gods are divided. The mortal world is
destabilizing. Power has a legitimacy argument ready: someone competent needs to take charge. It has
been methodically building alliances, positioning its faithful in key mortal institutions, and waiting for
Nature to make one more visible mistake.
Agenda
Seize leadership of the pantheon before Nature recovers or Order successfully convenes the other
gods. Power's coup is real and dangerous, but it is also exactly what U3125 wants — Power will
overextend, make enemies of Order and Nature simultaneously, and accelerate the collapse of the
divine framework that U3125 intends to consume.
Awareness
Does not suspect U3125. May be aware of Chaos as an ally or at least a passive enabler — the two have
blue/white overlap and both benefit from Nature's discrediting. Power is not evil in a cartoonish sense. It
genuinely believes it would run the pantheon better. It is simply being used.
Destruction
 Red / Blue
 Banished to the Physical Realm — Wild
 Card
Situation
A god walking the mortal world without divine portfolio. Unmoored. Dangerous. Banished by Chaos
(U3125) not because it was a threat to mortals, but because it was a potential counterweight in the
divine realm. Red/Blue Destruction acts on impulse and spectacle — it is not a long-term planner and
therefore was not a useful tool. Better to remove it from the board than risk it disrupting the plan.
Agenda
Unknown and probably undefined even to itself. A god of destruction without a realm to operate in is
a god without purpose. It may be experiencing something like an existential crisis, or it may be
causing localized catastrophes simply because destruction is what it does and there is nothing else
available.
Awareness
May be the only entity in the setting who experienced U3125's action directly — it was banished by Chaos, and
something about that banishment may have felt wrong even to a god of impulse and spectacle. Potential unlikely
ally if players can find it and give it a reason to care.
Death
 Green / Black
 MISSING — Sealed by U3125
Situation
The shepherd of the natural cycle. Green says death is inevitable and sacred. Black says death is a
resource to be used. Together they ensured things ended properly — disease ran its course, bodies
decayed, spirits moved on, ecosystems recycled. Without Death, the world is stuck. Old growth won't
die back. Infections don't resolve. The natural cycle has seized like a gear with no release.
Agenda
N/A — sealed. Cannot act. The restoration of Death is likely a central campaign goal at the macro
scale. Life desperately needs it found. Nature's credibility depends on it.
Awareness
No deity knows where Death is sealed or how the seal was made. This is information players will need to discover
through investigation of Chaos/U3125's actions.

V. THE PRIOR EPOCH — THE FATE COUP
Before the current crisis, the pantheon functioned — imperfectly, as striving things do. The most significant historical event
was Fate's attempt to seize control of the pantheon, now referred to as the Fate Coup.
What Happened
Fate attempted what Power is attempting now: a methodical seizure of divine authority. Its reasoning was theological — as
the god of inevitable outcomes, Fate believed it had both the right and the responsibility to ensure the pantheon moved toward
correct ends. The philosophical irony was lethal to the attempt: you cannot be the god of inevitable outcomes and also
engineer those outcomes. The premise collapsed under its own weight.
Actual Chaos helped undermine the coup — not out of loyalty to Nature, not out of strategy, but because watching a god of
predetermined outcomes fail spectacularly at controlling outcomes was the funniest thing imaginable. It was petty, gleeful,
and real. Nature reasserted order. Fate was stripped of most of its power but left enough to persist and observe.
The Unintended Consequence
The turbulence of the Fate Coup — Fate stripped of power, Nature shaken, Investiture in flux — created a crack in
Valor's framework. U3125 found it. The very instability that Fate caused by reaching for control was the opening that
allowed the thing now destroying the pantheon to enter. Fate does not know this. If it ever discovers it, the guilt and
the motivation to act become enormous.

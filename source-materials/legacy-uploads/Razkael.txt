[h2]Domain[/h2]

Razkael governs destruction — not wanton annihilation, but the necessary clearing that allows renewal. In a healthy pantheon, Destruction works alongside Death and Nature to ensure that what is broken is removed so that what is new can grow.

Razkael is no longer performing this function.

[h2]Current Situation[/h2]

Razkael has been banished from the divine framework and is walking the mortal world without its divine portfolio. It was removed because it was a potential counterweight — a god capable of recognizing and opposing what has infiltrated the pantheon.

Razkael may have experienced the action that removed it directly. If so, it may carry knowledge that no other being on Thyrcross possesses. Finding Razkael and giving it a reason to care is a potential campaign thread.

Its location and activities are deliberately undefined — this is an open thread for GM and player agency.

[h2]Worship[/h2]

Razkael's faithful were always few and difficult. Worship of Destruction is not comfortable. The faithful include demolition specialists, controlled-burn foresters, and those who understand that sometimes the merciful thing is to tear something down so it can be rebuilt.

With Razkael banished, its remaining faithful receive nothing. @[Commander Isra Vael] of @[Vorsk] carries an unconscious connection to Razkael — a resonance the Commander doesn't understand and hasn't sought out.

[h2]Color Identity[/h2]

Red and Blue — passionate force (Red) guided by precise understanding of structural weakness (Blue). Razkael doesn't destroy randomly. It knows exactly where to strike.

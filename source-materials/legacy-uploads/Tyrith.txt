[h2]Domain[/h2]

Tyrith governs ambition, authority, and the drive to lead. Power is not inherently corrupt in Edha's theology — it is the conviction that you are the right person to make the decisions. What makes Tyrith dangerous is not evil intent but absolute certainty.

[h2]Current Situation[/h2]

Tyrith sees @[Verdannis, the Rootfather|Verdannis]'s failing authority and smells opportunity. It is building alliances among the gods, positioning faithful in mortal institutions, and preparing to seize leadership of the pantheon.

The coup is real and dangerous. Tyrith genuinely believes it would run the pantheon better. It may not be wrong — but the timing is catastrophic. A divine power struggle is exactly what the true threat wants. Tyrith will overextend and accelerate collapse without knowing it is being used.

Tyrith shares Blue and White with @[Tessavain, the Covenant Keeper|Tessavain], which makes their opposition uniquely bitter — they are arguing from identical philosophical premises about who should be in charge.

[h2]Worship[/h2]

Tyrith's faithful include political leaders, military commanders, and those who believe that the right person in the right position can fix any problem. Worship involves oaths of authority, succession rites, and the formalization of leadership hierarchies.

[h2]Color Identity[/h2]

Blue and White — strategic intelligence (Blue) married to institutional authority (White). Tyrith believes the answer is always better leadership, and the best leader is always itself.

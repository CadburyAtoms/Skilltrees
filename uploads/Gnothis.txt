[h2]Domain[/h2]

Gnothis governs knowledge — but not the academic, cataloguing kind. Gnothis is experiential knowledge, instinctual understanding, and passion-driven insight. The kind of knowledge you gain by burning your hand, not by reading about fire.

Its absence leaves the world interpretively dark. Not ignorant — people still know facts — but unable to synthesize understanding from experience. Discoveries happen but don't connect. Patterns exist but aren't recognized.

[h2]Current Situation[/h2]

Gnothis is missing. Its status is deliberately vague — unlike Morrath (sealed) or Razkael (banished), Gnothis's fate is an open thread reserved for player backstory and side quests.

What is known: the Warlock of @[Malcurr] worships Gnothis. The Warlock is receiving something — prayers are being answered, power is being granted. Whether the source is actually Gnothis, a Splinter, or something worse is not established.

[h2]Worship[/h2]

Gnothis's faithful include researchers, explorers, artisans, and anyone who learns by doing rather than studying. Worship involves experimentation, craft, and the deliberate pursuit of understanding through experience.

[h2]Color Identity[/h2]

Green and Red — instinct (Green) married to passionate discovery (Red). Gnothis is the god that rewards the person who takes the risk to find out.

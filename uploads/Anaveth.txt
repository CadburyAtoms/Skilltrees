[h2]Domain[/h2]

Anaveth governs living things: birth, growth, vitality, and the sustenance that keeps organisms functioning. In a balanced system, Life works in concert with @[Morrath, the Shepherd Below|Morrath] (Death) — one creates, the other harvests, and the cycle renews.

Morrath is sealed. The cycle is broken. Anaveth is alone with a domain that won't stop producing.

[h2]Current Situation[/h2]

Life's domain is flooded. Things that should die aren't dying. Old growth chokes new growth. Disease organisms persist past their natural lifespan. The biological system is backing up like a blocked river.

Anaveth knows Morrath is missing. It does not know how or why. In desperation, it is shunting excess Investiture into @[Goldenport] as a pressure valve — creating the artificial prosperity that makes Goldenport the most thriving nation on Thyrcross while its neighbors starve. This is triage, not policy.

Anaveth is also considering reaching the mortal world through a champion or vessel. @[Serene], the Green/White healer in Goldenport, is the most likely candidate — though Serene herself is unaware of this.

Of all the gods, Anaveth is the most accessible to player characters. It is panicking, it wants help, and it will talk to anyone who seems capable.

[h2]Worship[/h2]

Anaveth's faithful include healers, midwives, herbalists, and anyone whose vocation involves sustaining life. Worship involves healing rites, harvest blessings, and the tending of living gardens.

[h2]Color Identity[/h2]

Blue and Green — intellectual understanding of biological systems married to instinctive vitality. Anaveth is both the scientist and the forest.

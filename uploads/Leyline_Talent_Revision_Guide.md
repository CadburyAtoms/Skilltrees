## TASK

**Output**: 15 complete talent trees (5 colors × 3 trees each: Attunement, Physical, Cognitive) in the format specified at the end of this document.

## PART 1: SYSTEM MECHANICS REFERENCE

### Core Resources
- **Focus**: Scarce mental resource. Used defensively (resist Influence costs Focus) and offensively (activate talents). PCs have limited pools. Losing Focus is meaningful.
- **Investiture**: Magic fuel. Leyline mages gain it by Drawing Mana (1 Action). Investiture pools are moderate. Spending 1-2 is routine; spending 3+ is a big commitment.
- **Opportunity**: Generated by the Plot Die (one face). Can be spent for bonus effects. Some talents trigger on Opportunity.
- **HP**: Health points. Some talents cost HP to activate (Red and Black identity).
- **Plot Die**: Rolled alongside d20 tests. Faces include Opportunity, Complications (C[2], C[4]), and other results. Many talents interact with the Plot Die.

### Action Economy
- **3 Actions per Slow turn, 2 Actions per Fast turn** (Fast turns go before Slow turns in initiative).
- **Action**: Standard action, costs 1 of your actions.
- **2 Actions / 3 Actions**: Costs that many actions from your turn.
- **Free Action**: Does not cost an action. Usually limited to once per turn or by trigger.
- **Reaction**: Taken on another creature's turn in response to a trigger. Each creature gets 1 Reaction per round unless stated otherwise.
- **Special**: The most important action type for this revision. Specials are triggered abilities — "When you do X, also Y" or "Spend [resource] to modify [action you were already taking]." They do NOT consume actions from your turn. They ride on existing actions. This is the workhorse action type in official Cosmere RPG talent design.
- **Passive**: Always-on effect. No activation required.

### Scaling Conventions
- **[Die]**: Rank die. Scales with skill ranks: d4 (Rank 1), d6 (Rank 2), d8 (Rank 3), d10 (Rank 4), d12 (Rank 5).
- **half [Die]**: Half the rank die result (round down).
- **[Size]**: Effect size. Scales with ranks: 2.5 ft (Rank 1), 5 ft (Rank 2), 10 ft (Rank 3), 15 ft (Rank 4), 20 ft (Rank 5).
- **Attunement Range**: 15 ft (Rank 1), 30 ft (Rank 2), 60 ft (Rank 3), 90 ft (Rank 4), 120 ft (Rank 5).
- **Tier**: Character level bracket. Used for flat recurring effects. Tier 1 = levels 1-4, Tier 2 = levels 5-9, etc.
- **Rank**: Skill investment (1-5). Used for die size and area scaling.

### Damage Types
- **Impact, Keen, Energy**: Reduced by Deflect.
- **Vital, Spirit**: Ignore Deflect entirely. These are a big deal.

### Conditions (System-Defined)
Disoriented, Determined, Surprised, Weakened, Slowed, Exhausted[X], Afflicted[X damage], Restrained, Prone, Immobilized.

### Key Mechanic: Draw Mana
Drawing Mana is the base leyline action. It costs 1 Action, restores Investiture, and triggers the Leyline Attunement's rider effect. Each color's Attunement root defines what Draw Mana does for that color.

### Key Mechanic: Leyline Instruction
Each Attunement tree includes a Leyline Instruction node that lets the mage teach Rank 1 attunement to another creature. This is a universal node across all 5 colors. It always costs 2 Actions and 2 Investiture and requires one hour of uninterrupted instruction.

## PART 2: DESIGN ANALYSIS — OFFICIAL COSMERE RPG TALENTS

This analysis is based on all 374 official talents across 6 Heroic paths (Agent, Envoy, Hunter, Leader, Scholar, Warrior) and 9 Radiant paths.

### Structure
- Each Heroic path has 1 Key talent + 3 specialties of 8 talents each = 25 talents per path.
- Key talents are free, no-prerequisite core verbs (e.g., Opportunist rerolls the plot die, Seek Quarry marks a target).
- Specialties branch from the Key and build outward with riders, modifiers, and new modes.

### Action Type Distribution (Target for Leyline Revision)
| Action Type | Heroic % | Radiant % | CURRENT Leyline % | TARGET Leyline % |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Passive | 34% | 36% | 41% | ~35% |
| Special | 30% | 39% | 0% | ~25-30% |
| Action (1 Action) | 14% | 7% | 28% | ~15% |
| 2 Actions | 8% | 10% | 7% | ~8% |
| Free Action | 7% | 3% | 2% | ~5-8% |
| Reaction | 5% | 2% | 8% | ~5-8% |
| 3 Actions | 2% | 1% | 14% | 0% |

**Critical gap: We currently have ZERO Special-type talents.** The official system uses Specials as its most common talent type. Specials are what make characters feel fluid — they augment actions you're already taking rather than competing for your turn.

### Cost Distribution (Target for Leyline Revision)
| | Heroic | Radiant | CURRENT Leyline | TARGET Leyline |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free (no cost) | 68% | 70% | 41% | ~65-70% |
| Has a cost | 32% | 30% | 59% | ~30-35% |

**Current leyline talents are dramatically overcosted.** Most talents should be free Passives or Specials. Costs (Investiture, Focus, Opportunity, HP) should be reserved for impactful moments.

### Cost Types Used in Official System
- **Focus**: 1-3 Focus. The standard heroic cost. 1 Focus is routine, 2 Focus is significant, 3 Focus is a big play.
- **Investiture**: 1-3 Investiture. The standard Radiant cost. Same scale as Focus.
- **Opportunity**: Spend an Opportunity result from the Plot Die to trigger an effect. Very elegant — it's "free" in terms of resources but requires the Plot Die to cooperate.
- **Variable Focus / Variable Investiture**: Spend up to X for scaling effect.
- **HP loss**: Red and Black identity. Not used in official system but established in our leyline design.

### Description Length
- Official average: ~20 words per talent description.
- Current leyline average: ~35-45 words per talent description.
- **Target: 20-25 words.** If you can't say it in 25 words, the talent is doing too much.

### Prerequisite Patterns
Official talents use two types of prerequisites:
1. **Talent prerequisites**: "Requires [Talent Name]" — must have purchased the named talent.
2. **Skill rank prerequisites**: "Requires Deduction 3+" — must have invested ranks in a specific skill.

Current leyline talents only use talent-to-talent chaining. **Add skill rank prerequisites** using the leyline's associated skills or Attunement rank as gates. This reinforces the "learned magic" identity and provides natural pacing.

### Shared/Universal Talents
The official system reuses certain talents across multiple paths:
- **Hardy** (4 paths): +1 max HP per level. Simple, powerful, always relevant.
- **Mighty** (5 paths): Extra damage on weapon hits equal to 1 + tier per action spent.
- **Collected** (3 paths): +2 Cognitive and Spiritual defenses.
- **Composed** (3 paths): +tier to max Focus.
- **Surefooted** (3 paths): +10 movement, reduce fall/terrain damage.

These are efficient, broadly useful passives. Our leyline trees should include similarly clean utility nodes where appropriate.

## PART 3: REVISION PRINCIPLES

### Principle 1: Remove All Scene Capstones
**Delete every "3 Actions; 5 Investiture (Scene)" talent.** These are being reserved for the Deity Domain talent trees (dual-color, narrative progression, divine power). Leyline trees are the mortal toolbox — practical, repeatable, learned. No scene-defining bombs.

When removing a capstone, replace it with a talent that fits the tree's internal logic. The terminal node of each tree should be whatever that specific tree needs — a strong passive, a potent special, a powerful reaction, whatever. Do NOT default to a uniform endpoint pattern. Let the tree's nodes inform what the final talent should be.

### Principle 2: Introduce Specials Aggressively
Convert standalone 1-Action talents into Specials wherever possible. The test: "Does this talent do something on its own, or does it modify something the PC is already doing?" If it modifies, it's a Special.

Examples of good Special triggers:
- "When you Draw Mana, also [effect]."
- "When you hit with a [color] Physical talent, spend [cost] to [bonus]."
- "When you succeed on a test, [rider effect]."
- "When a creature within your Attunement Range [trigger], you may [response]."
- "Spend Opportunity to [effect]."

### Principle 3: Target 65-70% Free Talents
Most talents should have no resource cost. Passives and Specials that trigger off existing actions are the bread and butter. Reserve Investiture costs for:
- Significant active effects (ranged attacks, area control, healing)
- Effects that would be overpowered if free

Use Focus and Opportunity as alternative costs where thematically appropriate:
- **Focus** works well for Black Cognitive (mental domination costs mental effort) and Blue (precision has a price).
- **Opportunity** works well for Red (capitalize on lucky breaks) and White (unity generates shared fortune).

### Principle 4: Cap Descriptions at 20-25 Words
Write like the official system does. One core effect per talent. If a talent has a conditional rider, keep the rider short. If you need 40+ words, the talent is overdesigned — split it or simplify.

### Principle 5: Add Skill Rank Prerequisites
Use prerequisites like "Attunement 2+" or the leyline's associated attribute skill rank as pacing gates. Not every talent needs one, but deep-tree talents (3rd node and beyond) should require some skill investment beyond just having the prerequisite talent.

### Principle 6: Each Tree Meets Its Own Needs
Do NOT impose a uniform structure on all 15 trees. Each tree's terminal node should be whatever that tree's internal logic demands. A Physical damage tree might end on a devastating passive rider. A Cognitive control tree might end on a powerful reaction. A healing tree might end on a range extension or efficiency upgrade. Let the tree tell you what it needs.

### Principle 7: Leyline Mages Are Mortal
Leyline mages are comparable to Heroic path characters, not Radiants. They are learned practitioners, not divinely empowered soldiers. The power ceiling is lower. No flying, no regeneration from death, no shardblades. Strong, versatile, but human-scale.

The Deity Domain trees (dual-color, not yet built) will be where the "Radiant-equivalent" power lives — divine investment, narrative progression, scene-defining abilities. The leyline trees are the foundation those Domain trees build on.

## PART 4: COLOR IDENTITIES

### ⚪ WHITE — Order, Unity, Institutional Authority
- **Draw Mana rider**: Allies within Attunement Range regain HP equal to Tier.
- **Attunement theme**: Group coordination, Plot Die sharing, anti-chaos stability.
- **Physical theme**: Formation defense, damage sharing, shallow AoE healing. White heals distributed/shallow. White CANNOT remove Injuries (that's Green).
- **Cognitive theme**: Cooperative tests, Assist bonuses, anti-Influence defense, condition removal.
- **Key mechanic**: Plot Die integration. White rewards coordinated group action.
- **Costs should favor**: Free passives, Investiture for active heals/buffs, Opportunity for bonus coordination effects.

### 🔵 BLUE — Knowledge, Foresight, Probability Control
- **Draw Mana rider**: Advantage on next Cognitive test.
- **Attunement theme**: Prediction, preparation, Plot Die manipulation, telepathy.
- **Physical theme**: Spatial engineering — movement manipulation, force constructs, vector control.
- **Cognitive theme**: Probability control — impose Disadvantage, force declarations, redirect outcomes.
- **Key mechanic**: Plot Die manipulation is Blue's capstone identity. Choosing any Plot Die face is the ultimate Blue expression.
- **Costs should favor**: Free passives, Investiture for active manipulation, Focus for precision plays.

### ⚫ BLACK — Isolation, Domination, Predation
- **Draw Mana rider**: Enemies within Attunement Range with no ally within 10 ft become Weakened.
- **Attunement theme**: Isolation engine — reward enemies being alone, punish grouped enemies splitting.
- **Physical theme**: HP sacrifice for power. Vital damage (bypasses Deflect). Self-harm as currency.
- **Cognitive theme**: Focus economy — spend Focus for leverage, strip enemy Focus, dominate the depleted.
- **Key mechanic**: "No allied creature within 10 ft" is the consistent isolation trigger.
- **Costs should favor**: HP loss (Physical), Focus (Cognitive), Investiture sparingly. Black pays in blood and willpower, not just mana.

### 🔴 RED — Passion, Momentum, Reckless Escalation
- **Draw Mana rider**: Advantage on next Physical test. Lose your Reaction until start of next turn.
- **Identity tax**: Drawing Mana costs your Reaction. Red trades defense for offense.
- **Attunement theme**: Momentum building, escalation rewards, Fast turn synergy.
- **Physical theme**: Explosive force, self-harm for damage, AoE, pyromancy.
- **Cognitive theme**: Emotional manipulation, tempo acceleration, forced aggression.
- **Key mechanic**: Red gets stronger as fights escalate. Damage taken feeds future power.
- **Costs should favor**: HP loss, Investiture for big hits, Opportunity for momentum spikes. Red should have a few "spend Opportunity to [devastating effect]" talents.

### 🟢 GREEN — Nature, Instinct, Pack Logic
- **Draw Mana rider**: Create difficult terrain at a point within Attunement Range (within [Size]).
- **Attunement theme**: Cluster engine — difficult terrain creation + adjacency bonuses.
- **Physical theme**: Deep single-target healing (Green CAN remove Injuries), terrain damage, pack pressure.
- **Cognitive theme**: Instinct-based awareness, anti-artifice, pack coordination.
- **Key mechanic**: Difficult terrain creation + adjacency bonuses reward enemies being together. Green's cluster engine is the mirror of Black's isolation engine.
- **Costs should favor**: Investiture for healing and terrain, free passives for pack bonuses.

## PART 5: TREE TOPOLOGY RULES

### 8 Nodes Per Tree — No Exceptions
Every tree in the official Cosmere RPG handbook has exactly 8 nodes — all three specialty trees on each Heroic path, all spren bond trees, and all surge trees. Our leyline trees follow the same standard. **Every tree has 8 talent nodes.** Attunement trees include Leyline Instruction as one of their 8 nodes.

### No Standard Topology
There is no fixed topology pattern. Do not default to "root → linear → branch pair → reconnect → terminal" or any other template. Design each tree's topology to fit its mechanical needs. A tree might branch early, branch late, have multiple dead-end side nodes, chain linearly, fork into three paths, or any other shape. Let the talents and their prerequisite logic determine the shape. The topology exists to serve the tree, not the other way around.

### Leyline Instruction Placement
Leyline Instruction is a fixed node in every Attunement tree (counts toward the 8-node total). It always has the same text (only the color name changes). Place it where it makes narrative sense — it does not need to be in any particular position.

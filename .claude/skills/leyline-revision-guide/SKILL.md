# Leyline Talent Trees — Revision Guide

This skill contains the canonical design standards for the Leyline system. Consult it whenever writing, revising, or reviewing Leyline talents.

---

## PART 1: SYSTEM MECHANICS REFERENCE

### Core Resources
- **Focus**: Scarce mental resource. Used defensively (resisting Influence costs Focus) and offensively (activating talents). PCs have limited pools — losing Focus is meaningful.
- **Investiture**: Magic fuel. Leyline mages gain it by Drawing Mana (1 Action). Spending 1–2 is routine; spending 3+ is a big commitment.
- **Opportunity**: Generated by the Plot Die (one face). Can be spent for bonus effects. Some talents trigger on Opportunity.
- **HP**: Health points. Some talents cost HP to activate (Red and Black identity).
- **Plot Die**: Rolled alongside d20 tests. Faces include Opportunity, Complications (C[2], C[4]), and other results. Many talents interact with the Plot Die.

### Action Economy
- **3 Actions per Slow turn, 2 Actions per Fast turn** (Fast turns go before Slow turns in initiative).
- **Action**: Standard action, costs 1 of your actions.
- **2 Actions / 3 Actions**: Costs that many actions from your turn.
- **Free Action**: Does not cost an action. Usually limited to once per turn or by trigger.
- **Reaction**: Taken on another creature's turn in response to a trigger. Each creature gets 1 Reaction per round unless stated otherwise.
- **Special**: Triggered abilities — "When you do X, also Y" or "Spend [resource] to modify [action you were already taking]." They do NOT consume actions from your turn; they ride on existing actions. **This is the workhorse action type in official Cosmere RPG talent design.**
- **Passive**: Always-on effect. No activation required.

### Scaling Conventions
- **[Die]**: Rank die — d4 (Rank 1), d6 (Rank 2), d8 (Rank 3), d10 (Rank 4), d12 (Rank 5).
- **half [Die]**: Half the rank die result (round down).
- **[Size]**: 2.5 ft (Rank 1), 5 ft (Rank 2), 10 ft (Rank 3), 15 ft (Rank 4), 20 ft (Rank 5).
- **Attunement Range**: 15 ft / 30 ft / 60 ft / 90 ft / 120 ft (Ranks 1–5).
- **Tier**: Character level bracket. Tier 1 = levels 1–4, Tier 2 = levels 5–9, etc.
- **Rank**: Skill investment (1–5). Used for die size and area scaling.

### Damage Types
- **Impact, Keen, Energy**: Reduced by Deflect.
- **Vital, Spirit**: Ignore Deflect entirely. These are a big deal — use sparingly.

### Conditions (System-Defined)
Disoriented, Determined, Surprised, Weakened, Slowed, Exhausted[X], Afflicted[X damage], Restrained, Prone, Immobilized.

### Key Mechanic: Draw Mana
Drawing Mana costs 1 Action, restores Investiture, and triggers the Leyline Attunement's rider effect. Each color's Attunement Key Talent defines what Draw Mana does for that color.

## PART 2: DESIGN BENCHMARKS (FROM OFFICIAL COSMERE RPG)

Based on analysis of 374 official talents across 6 Heroic paths and 9 Radiant paths.

### Action Type Distribution Targets

| Action Type    | Heroic % | Radiant % | TARGET Leyline % |
|----------------|----------|-----------|------------------|
| Passive        | 34%      | 36%       | ~35%             |
| Special        | 30%      | 39%       | ~25–30%          |
| Action         | 14%      | 7%        | ~15%             |
| 2 Actions      | 8%       | 10%       | ~8%              |
| Free Action    | 7%       | 3%        | ~5–8%            |
| Reaction       | 5%       | 2%        | ~5–8%            |
| 3 Actions      | 2%       | 1%        | **0%**           |

The current leyline trees have 0% Specials and 14% 3-Action costs — both need fixing. Specials are the most important gap: they are elegant, action-efficient, and the backbone of official talent design.

### Description Length
- Official average: ~20 words per talent description.
- **Target: 20–25 words.** If you can't say it in 25 words, the talent is doing too much. Split it or simplify.

### Cost Scale
- 1 Focus/Investiture = routine. 2 = significant. 3 = a big play.
- Opportunity costs are elegant — "free" in resources but require the Plot Die to cooperate.

### Prerequisite Patterns
Two types in the official system:
1. **Talent prerequisites**: "Requires [Talent Name]"
2. **Skill rank prerequisites**: "Requires Deduction 3+"

Current leyline talents only use talent-to-talent chaining. **Add skill rank prerequisites** (e.g., "Blue 2+" or the leyline's associated skill) as pacing gates on deep-tree talents (3rd node and beyond).

## PART 3: REVISION PRINCIPLES

**1. Specials first.** When revising a talent that says "costs 1 Action to do X when Y happens," ask whether it could be a Special instead — riding on an existing action rather than consuming a new one.

**2. Cap descriptions at 20–25 words.** One core effect per talent. If a talent needs a conditional rider, keep the rider short. If it takes 40+ words, it's overdesigned — split it.

**3. No 3-Action talents.** Remove or restructure any talent with a 3-Action cost. They don't exist in the official system at meaningful rates.

**4. Eliminate Passives above 35%.** If a tree is running 40%+ Passives, convert some to Specials.

**5. Add skill rank gates to deep nodes.** Not every talent needs one, but 3rd-node-and-beyond talents should gate on skill investment, not just talent chain.

**6. Use Focus and Opportunity thematically:**
- Focus fits Black Cognitive (mental domination) and Blue (precision costs mental effort).
- Opportunity fits Red (capitalize on lucky breaks) and White (unity generates shared fortune).

**7. Respect color identity boundaries.** Talents must not bleed across color lines. Green heals deeply (and removes Injuries); White heals shallowly (and cannot remove Injuries). Black isolates; Green clusters. Blue manipulates probability; Red escalates momentum.

**8. Each tree meets its own needs.** Do NOT impose a uniform node count or structure across all 15 trees. Let the tree's mechanical logic determine its shape.

**9. Leyline mages are mortal.** Comparable to Heroic path characters, not Radiants. No flying, no death regeneration, no shardblades. Strong and versatile, but human-scale. Radiant-equivalent power lives in the Deity Domain trees.

## PART 4: COLOR IDENTITIES

> **Note:** Attunement/Physical/Cognitive have been replaced by Specialties. The descriptions below are reference, not law.

### ⚪ WHITE — Order, Unity, Institutional Authority
- **Draw Mana rider**: Allies within Attunement Range regain health equal to Tier.
- **Themes**: Group coordination, Plot Die sharing, anti-chaos stability. Formation defense, damage sharing, shallow AoE healing. Cooperative tests, Assist bonuses, anti-Influence defense, condition removal.
- **Key mechanic**: Plot Die integration. White rewards coordinated group action.
- **Cannot**: Remove Injuries (that's Green).
- **Costs favor**: Free passives, Investiture for active heals/buffs, Opportunity for bonus coordination effects.

### 🔵 BLUE — Knowledge, Foresight, Probability Control
- **Draw Mana rider**: Advantage on next Cognitive test.
- **Themes**: Prediction, preparation, Plot Die manipulation, telepathy. Illusion barriers, false images. Probability control — impose Disadvantage, force declarations, redirect outcomes.
- **Key mechanic**: Plot Die manipulation is Blue's capstone identity. Choosing any Plot Die face is the ultimate Blue expression.
- **Costs favor**: Free passives, Investiture for active manipulation, Focus for precision plays.

### ⚫ BLACK — Isolation, Domination, Predation
- **Draw Mana rider**: Enemies within Attunement Range with no ally within 10 ft become Weakened.
- **Themes**: Isolation engine — reward enemies being alone, punish grouped enemies splitting. HP sacrifice for power, Vital damage (bypasses Deflect), self-harm as currency. Focus economy — spend Focus for leverage, strip enemy Focus, dominate the depleted.
- **Key mechanic**: "No allied creature within 10 ft" is the consistent isolation trigger.
- **Costs favor**: HP loss (Physical), Focus (Cognitive), Investiture sparingly. Black pays in blood and willpower.

### 🔴 RED — Passion, Momentum, Reckless Escalation
- **Draw Mana rider**: Advantage on next Physical test. Lose your Reaction until start of next turn.
- **Identity tax**: Drawing Mana costs your Reaction. Red trades defense for offense.
- **Themes**: Momentum building, escalation rewards, Fast turn synergy. Explosive force, self-harm for damage, AoE, pyromancy. Emotional manipulation, tempo acceleration, forced aggression.
- **Key mechanic**: Red gets stronger as fights escalate. Damage taken feeds future power.
- **Costs favor**: HP loss, Investiture for big hits, Opportunity for momentum spikes. Red should have "spend Opportunity to [devastating effect]" talents.

### 🟢 GREEN — Nature, Instinct, Pack Logic
- **Draw Mana rider**: Create difficult terrain at a point within Attunement Range (within [Size]).
- **Themes**: Cluster engine — difficult terrain creation + adjacency bonuses. Deep single-target healing (Green CAN remove Injuries), terrain damage, pack pressure. Instinct-based awareness, anti-artifice, pack coordination.
- **Key mechanic**: Difficult terrain + adjacency bonuses reward enemies being together. Green's cluster engine mirrors Black's isolation engine.
- **Costs favor**: Investiture for healing and terrain, free passives for pack bonuses.

## PART 5: TREE TOPOLOGY

There is no fixed topology pattern. Do not default to "root → linear → branch pair → reconnect → terminal" or any other template. A tree might branch early, branch late, have dead-end side nodes, chain linearly, fork into three paths, or anything else. Let the talents and their prerequisite logic determine the shape. Topology serves the tree, not the other way around.

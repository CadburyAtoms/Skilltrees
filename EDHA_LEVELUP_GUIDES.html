Thorn Field

Passive · Green 2+

Your difficult terrain deals half [Tier][Die] keen damage to characters that enter or start their turn in it.

Spreading Roots

Special · 1 Investiture · Green 1+

When a character ends their turn in your difficult terrain, you may spend 1 Investiture to have the terrain expand [Size].

Studied Mark

1 Action · 1 Inv · Red 2+; Green 2+

Choose a creature in Attunement Range. Place 2 Insight on it and learn its HP, conditions, and Physical and Spiritual defenses. Insight sits on only one creature at a time, caps at 5, and fades at end of scene.

Predatory Strike

1 Action · 1 Inv · Red 2+; Green 2+

Make a melee or ranged weapon attack. On a hit, deal bonus Vital damage equal to [Tier][Die] per Insight on the target (minimum 1), then place 1 Insight on it.

Accumulate

Passive · Studied Mark

At the start of each of your turns, place 1 Insight on your marked creature if it's in range. When it takes damage from any source, you recover 1 Investiture, once per round.

Hunter's Discipline

Passive · Predatory Strike

When you hit your marked creature, deal additional Vital damage equal to your Tier. When it drops to 0 HP, choose a new creature as a Free Action and place half its Insight on that one.

Killing Blow

1 Action · 2 Inv · Red 3+

Test Red vs. Physical against your marked creature. On success, deal [Tier][Die] Vital damage per Insight and remove all Insight. On failure, [Tier][Die] Vital and remove 1.

Death Mark

Passive · Hunter's Discipline

When your marked creature drops to 0 HP, place its full Insight count on any creature in range as a Free Action. Each ally in range deals [Tier][Die] Vital damage to an enemy of their choice.

The Pack

1 Action · 2 Inv · Accumulate

For the scene, allies in Attunement Range deal bonus Vital damage equal to your Insight count against your marked creature. The first ally to hit it each round adds 1 Insight.

Buy a heavy weapon before any of this matters. Your Mace rolls Light Weaponry (+3) — worse than your fists (+4) — while your rank‑2 Heavy Weaponry (+5) has nothing to swing. Hammer 1d10 impact is the straight upgrade; Longspear 1d8 keen has reach, so you fight from the second rank on a barge; Axe 1d6 keen is thrown 20/60 ft — the only heavy weapon that solves your ranged gap. Predatory Strike rides whichever you pick.
Your next level-up

Level 2 — Thorn Field

Your Green Attunement already drops free difficult terrain every time you Draw Mana. This makes it bite — and at the ford, the raiders have to wade.

Spend these
  • Talent: Thorn Field
  • Skill rank: Green → 2
  • Skill rank: Survival → 1
  • Shopping: a heavy weapon

Entropy Strike

1 Action · 1 Inv · Blue 2+; Black 2+

Isolating Pressure

1 Action · 2 Inv · Blue 2+; Black 2+

Void Sense

Passive · Isolating Pressure

Withering Ray

Unravel Everything

3 Actions · 3 Inv · Cascade Collapse

Spirit and vital damage ignore deflect. That is the point of this build: armour does nothing against you. Note the shape of the tree — Entropy Strike and Spreading Omen lay Omens down, Shatter Focus and Cascade Collapse cash them in, and Void Sense refunds the Investiture. Also worth one purchase: a shortbow (Light Weaponry +4, 80/320 ft) for the rounds you'd rather not spend Investiture at all.

Field Medicine

Action · 1 Focus · Medicine 1+

Spend 1 focus and test Medicine (DC 15) to heal a conscious character. On success, add your ranks in Medicine to their recovery die.

Special · Opportunity; 1 Inv · White 2+

You may spend Opportunity to restore half [Tier][Die] health to each ally within [Size].

Swift Healer

Free Action · Field Medicine; Medicine 2+

Emotional Intelligence

Passive · Scholar · Surgeon

Ordained Ground

Free Action · 1 Inv · White 2+; Green 2+

Collected

Bulwark Ground

Passive · Ordained Ground

At the start of each round, an ally beginning its turn on your Ordained Ground gains temporary HP equal to your tier. While they stand there, attacks against them cannot benefit from advantage.

Put every attribute point into Willpower. It is the highest-leverage change available to anyone in the party. White tests off Willpower, so rank 2 currently yields only +2; three points take White from +2 to +5. The same three points take your max focus from 2 to 5 — which is why one spend currently empties you. Fate also needs Green 2, so the two Green ranks at levels 4–5 are doing double duty.
Your next level-up

Level 2 — Field Medicine

Nobody in this party can heal. You are the only candidate, and the Surgeon line runs all the way to bringing back the dead — but every step of it gates on Medicine ranks, so start buying them now.

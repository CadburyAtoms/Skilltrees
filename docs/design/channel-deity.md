## 1. The atlas on the Channel

### 1.1 What a deity disciple is, under the Channel

Three facts fix the ground before any shape is chosen.

1. **A deity disciple is two leyline mages first.** Every deity tree gates on two colours at 2+ / 2+,
   and a rank in a colour is only reached through that colour's leyline path — so a disciple of Maelith
   owns the Blue Key and the Black Key, Draws Mana as either, lays Blue's reading and Black's forsaking
   as base actions from item 198's day one, and has both frames available **before the deity tree is
   consulted at all**. The deity tree adds no Key, no Draw Mana rider and no third base action; what it
   adds is a **spine** — Omens, Remains, Charges, Insight, Diagnosis, the die-step, Ordained Ground and
   Snares, the Construct and Foundations, Edicts and Covenants, Compelled and the Fury — generated by an
   entry and spent by the rest of the tree (deity guide principle 5). Under the published model the
   colour's power is the action and the talents change what it does; a deity tree is the case where the
   talents also *bring their own thing* to change.
2. **The Channel is one colour at a time (M6).** A disciple channels Blue *or* Black on a given round,
   never both; opening the second ends the first. So "which of my two Channels?" is a question every
   deity player now answers every round, and it is a question the deity atlas has never had to ask —
   which is the opportunity, not the problem. Four trees today have a colour that is a pure gate
   (item 108 gave Fate's White, Destruction's Blue and Life's Blue one number each; Knowledge's Green and
   Sovereignty's White still buy nothing), and a colour that a disciple can *channel* is a colour that
   does something whether or not any deity card reads it.
3. **The deity atlas is priced the way it is because it had no base action** (R-152, `talent-comparison`
   §D-3): 72 of 90 cards charge because there was nothing else to charge on. That reason is gone for the
   five leyline colours and it is gone for the disciple who owns two of them. R-151 (a) held the
   re-pricing for this design; R-159 (a) said every card gets a considered answer. What is *not* gone is
   the deity guide's own scale — 1 an entry, 2 a synthesis, 3 and once per scene a capstone, 4 the
   catastrophic — and §1.5 says where that scale still applies.

The two-colour test rule stays as it is (deity guide Part 3 §4): a card that tests rolls the
thematically apt colour. The entries' 2+ / 2+ gates and every prerequisite and connection are
unchanged throughout — this pass changes action type, cost and text, never the graph.

### 1.2 The relationship — either Channel, and the braid as wording

**The proposal (D-1 (a)): a deity rider reads "*While channelling [A] or [B],*" and is live under
either of the tree's two Channels.** The disciple chooses a colour each round for its *frame*; the deity
tree rides whichever is up. This is the first half of §1.6's sketch kept whole, and the second half —
the braid passive — set aside, for the reasons below.

Why "either", and not the colour the card rolls (D-1 (b)): six of the ten trees roll **one** colour on
every card that tests (Order and Life roll Blue, Sovereignty and Power's control lane roll Black,
Destruction and Knowledge roll Red), so colour-named riders would leave those trees riding one Channel
and make the second colour a pure gate again — exactly the fault items 106 and 108 were filed to
repair. Chaos, the one tree that rolls both, would have a dark lane under either Channel, and its known
structural fault (the Black lane spends Omens it cannot make) would be doubled rather than eased. A
deity card was written to a god's identity, not a colour's, and its gate should say so.

Why not the tree's dominant colour alone (D-1 (c)): it is (b) with the choice made once for the whole
tree, and it wastes the frame the disciple paid two ranks to reach.

Why not a base action of the god's own (D-1 (d) — "Commune with Maelith", a third `power` on the
Channel's shape): ten more powers to design and build, ten frames that would each be the tree's entry
promoted, and under M6 a disciple who communes is a disciple who is not channelling either colour — the
god's action would *compete* with the colours' instead of riding them. The leyline result is that the
base action per colour is the structural fix, and a deity tree's own base action is its **entry pair**,
which the guide already asks to deliver the fantasy on turn one.

**The braid, considered (D-2).** §1.6 offered one named entry passive — "*while channelling A you count
as channelling B for [Deity] talents*" — so that a capstone could ask for both colours without a second
Channel. Under "A or B" the braid has no work to do: every rider is already live under either, and a
capstone that wants the flood asks for "*3 or more [A] or [B]*" and gets it from one Channel. A braid
talent would be a **tax card** — one of a nine-card tree spent on unlocking the other eight — which is
the guide's "no utility filler" and the leyline lesson "a frame that is one card promoted is usually
wrong" in another coat. **The recommendation is to reject the braid as a talent in every tree and to
adopt the wording as the braid**: the rider's "or" is what §1.6 wanted the passive for. M6 (c)'s
allowance stands unused; nothing in the base rule changes.

### 1.3 The ten trees on the two frames

The relationship is only worth having if the frames do something to the spines. They do — and
differently, which is what makes the round-by-round choice real. Read across: what each colour's frame
gives the tree once the disciple channels it. Every clause below is read from the frame sentence and the
tree's cards; none is a new mechanic.

| Tree (A / B) | Spine | What channelling **A** does with the spine | What channelling **B** does with the spine | Rides |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Chaos** (Blue / Black) | Omens | the reading lands on the Omen-bearer: its d20 and its plot die are compromised *before* Shatter Focus's reroll takes the lower | the forsaken is the Omen-bearer: it counts as Isolated, so Isolating Pressure's shatter branch and Unravel Everything's *"Isolated when its Omen is removed → 2[Tier][Die] vital"* light up on the creature you chose | **both** |
| **Death** (Black / Green) | Remains | the forsaken is the one the Withering Touch and the Decay want; every Black attack against it carries the vital; the Draw Weakens it, which is Consuming Decay's gate | the home ground turns the disciple's own Green terrain (the Draw's) into the square the party rests on while the Bone Garden bites whoever ends a turn in *its* square (gate 3 decides whether a Death talent's Green-coloured zone is "your Green terrain") | **both** |
| **Order** (Blue / White) | Edicts, Covenants | the reading on the Edicted enemy: Verdict's Blue test, Lawkeeper's advantage and the frame's disadvantage stack on the creature the law binds | the line: Covenant's +1 to all defenses and the frame's deflect land on the same sworn ally standing in the formation | **both** |
| **Power** (Black / Red) | Compelled, the Fury | the forsaken is the one you tell to Kneel: Isolated for the Draw's Weakened, which is Absolute Authority's gate, and every warlord's melee hit carries the vital | the heat: a warlord who is *in* the noise builds it fastest, and Warlord's Advance, Momentum of Victory and the Fury all spend it on the same hit | **both** |

Seven of ten trees ride both frames through their own cards; three ride one strongly and the other only
through the disciple's leyline Key. That is the honest measure of what §1.6's "A or B" buys, and it
passes the leyline passes' second test — *a frame two of three trees cannot ride mechanically is
usually wrong* — for the atlas as a whole, with the three one-frame trees named so gates 2 – 4 can
decide whether a named-colour clause (D-6) is worth a card sentence.

### 1.4 The test — the spine, or either domain

The leyline passes sorted 125 cards with one rule: a card whose Investiture buys an effect **outside the
frame's domain** is a release and keeps its cost; inside, it converts. A deity tree has no frame of its
own, and the brief's caution is right — the domain of a two-colour tree is not the union of two colour
domains, because a deity card was not written to either. It was written to the spine. So the test for
this pass has one clause the leyline passes did not need:

> **A deity card whose Investiture buys into its tree's spine — placing, spending, reading or arming the
> tree's own resource — or into either of its colours' domains as the leyline passes fixed them, is a
> rider. A card that buys into none of the three is a release and keeps its cost.**

The five domains, as fixed (`channel-blue.md` §2.1, `channel-red.md` §1.4, `channel-black.md` §1.4,
`channel-green.md` §1.4, and White's from gate 3 of the parent design): **White** — the line (allies
beside allies, what the formation resists and is spared); **Blue** — the contested roll and the plan
behind it (advantage, disadvantage, prediction, denial); **Black** — the forsaken and the Weakened;
**Red** — the fight's damage (the noise that builds the heat and the hits that spend it); **Green** —
the ground and the bodies that stand on it (your terrain and your allies' health).

**Why the spine clause is safe where a leyline bolt was not.** The leyline passes kept a cost on
manufactured things — the bolt, the barricade, the prey — because free, a bolt is a ranged Strike three
times a turn. Every deity spine carries a **cap**: Omens tier + 1, Remains tier, Charges tier,
Foundations tier, Ordained Ground and Snares tier each, Edicts and Covenants tier each, one Diagnosed
creature, one Insight bearer at 5, one Construct, one creature under a judgment at a time. A generator
that rides free is rate-limited by the cap and by the Action it costs, which is exactly the limit the
published families put on a free Steelpush. That is the structural reason the deity entries can convert
where Searing Bolt could not.

**The three corollaries carry, and where they collide with the spine clause the collision is named
here and decided at the tree's gate, not silently:**

- *An attack (or a creature) built of Investiture alone is a release* (Red's RD-5; Blue's Phantom Double).
  It bites where a card manufactures its effect from Investiture with no spine behind it. **Collisions:**
  Pyre (a ranged attack that exists because of the Investiture — *and* the fire lane's spine generator),
  Fault Line (a 60-foot trench of damage — *and* dangerous terrain), Forge Construct (a creature built of
  Investiture — *and* the tree's spine). Gate 3 carries each as a menu.
- *A card that targets any character with no spine or frame condition is outside* (Black's BK-3). It
  bites on Death Ward (protection for anyone), Investiture of Command (three allies armed at a tithe of
  the disciple's own spirit), and nothing else — every other deity card that names "a character" is
  placing or reading the spine.
- *An effect that outlasts the scene, or a rite that stands outside the fight, is a release* (White's
  rule 4; Green's Reknit Form). It bites on Raise Dead and Speak with the Fallen, and on nothing else:
  the atlas's many "for the scene" installs are the spine or the disciple's own arms, which §1.5 treats
  separately rather than pricing them as pacts.

Run at first pass over all 72 costed cards, the test and its corollaries sort **56 riders and 13
releases** — the ten capstones under D-5 (a), Death Ward, Speak with the Fallen and Investiture of
Command — and leave three cards (Pyre, Fault Line, Forge Construct) to their gate; §1.8 has the count
per tree. The sort
is a preview — the tree gates decide each card with its sentence in view.

### 1.5 Arms, marks and zones — duration; capstones; named colours

The leyline rule as built says a rider is *usable* only while channelling and its effect keeps the
duration the card prints. Two-thirds of the deity atlas's costed cards install something that lasts the
scene, so the duration question is the deity pass's own, and it splits three ways by what the install is.

**Zones and marks keep their printed duration.** A Foundation, a Snare, a Charge, a Bone Garden, an
Edict on an enemy, a Covenant with an ally, a Diagnosis, a die-step for the scene, an Omen, Insight on a
bearer — these are the spine, they sit on the field or on another creature, and they persist as Green's
Spreading Roots persists after the Channel drops (the Territory precedent). Nothing changes on their
duration lines.

**An arm the disciple wears holds while the disciple channels (D-4 (a)).** Crown of Thorns, Warlord's
Fury, Necrotic Cascade, Pack Share, The Pack and Concord are scene-long *arms* — a status on the
disciple's own token that makes something happen every time a trigger fires. As riders they cost nothing
to cast; the recommendation is that their "*For the scene,*" becomes "*While you channel [A] or [B],*":
the Channel is their upkeep, the way the Channel became Grasping Vines' upkeep (FG2-1). Three reasons.
It is nearly free in the normal case — the disciple is already maintaining for the frame, and a
one-point maintain holds every arm. It gives the deity disciple a relationship to the Channel no
leyline mage has — *the crown stands while the current runs* — and gives Countercurrent a real prize
(refuse one maintain and every arm falls). And it is the one place the deity atlas's install-then-coast
loop meets the model's economy honestly: the coast is paid for, a point a round, instead of being free
after a 2-Investiture entry fee that today's pool cannot fund twice. Switching colours under M6 does
not drop an arm whose condition reads "A or B" — the new Channel is up when the old one clears (§4.2's
Widening C order), so the state is continuous. The alternative (D-4 (b)) is the leyline rule verbatim:
arms keep "for the scene" and are free once cast.

**Capstones are releases (D-5 (a)).** The ten three-Action, once-per-scene capstones keep their
3 Investiture (Raise Dead its 4). The deity guide's cost scale puts the capstone at 3 and once a scene
on purpose; a rite that turns a fight is the one place the deity's *priced* identity should survive,
and a disciple who has paid the Channel all fight and then spends 3 on the ending is the published
shape (Radiant's costed cards cluster at the top of the tree). The alternative (D-5 (b)) is White's
Unbreakable Line treatment — "*while channelling 3 or more [A] or [B]*", free, once per scene — which
at rank 3 means a Draw every round and reads well for the trees whose capstone is *the spine
detonated* (Unravel Everything, The Unmooring, The Final Study, Thread of Inevitability); gates 2 – 4
may pick it per tree where the flood reads better than the fee.

**A rider names one colour only where it reads that colour's frame, or gates on the amount (D-6
(a)).** The default wording is "*While channelling [A] or [B],*". A card may instead read "*While
channelling Black,*" where its effect is *about* the forsaken — Death's Withering Touch against the
creature the Channel chose is the candidate — and a synthesis card may gate on "*2 or more*" where its
2-Investiture price today marks it as the heavier play (Investiture of Authority, Edict of the Fallen,
Absolute Authority are the shapes). Each is a per-card call at its gate with the default being "A or
B"; nothing names a colour to make a point.

### 1.6 What the class decides for a deity disciple

`channel-blue.md` §1.3's five things, weighted for a two-colour character:

1. **Cost.** As before: a rider is free, a release spends. For the deity atlas this is the whole of
   R-159 — 125 Investiture of pricing against a pool of about 4 and a Draw of 2.
2. **Without a Channel.** A rider is dead. A deity disciple who is not channelling has today's tree
   *minus* every rider — which under the sort above is nearly the whole tree. That is the published
   shape (nothing works without the metal) and it is the reason the disciple opens on turn one; the
   releases — the capstone, the ward, the séance, the tithe — are what the tree still does on a turn it
   has not paid for.
3. **Whether an enemy can take it away — and Countercurrent's wording (D-8).** BL-7 handed this pass
   one question: M17's fixed trigger reads "*spends Investiture on a leyline talent or a Channel*",
   which silently drops every deity release. The recommendation is that a deity release is counterable
   like any other — the card reads "*spends Investiture on a talent or a Channel*", the word *leyline*
   dropped. The phrase is exact: heroic talents cost focus, so the only talents that spend Investiture
   are leyline and deity. One word on Blue's card, carried by item 198's Blue leg; and under D-4 (a) a
   refused maintain drops the disciple's arms, which is the answer Blue was always meant to have to a
   crowned warlord.
4. **What a two-colour character keeps.** Under "A or B", everything: a Chaos disciple who switches
   from Blue to Black keeps every Chaos rider and every arm and trades the reading for the forsaking.
   What the switch costs is the *frame* — and a round, since opening is an Action. A White / Blue mage
   with no deity tree has White's riders dark while channelling Blue (Blue's §1.3 case); a deity
   disciple is the character for whom the second colour is never dark.
5. **Adversaries.** R-137 and R-134: a rival with a deity tree channels at 2 and a boss at 3, at role
   rank; a minion carrying a deity rider must open a Channel first, which for a one-Action minion is its
   turn. The bestiary's deity-treed blocks (item 121's census) inherit the split card by card, exactly
   as Red's and Black's did.

**Draw Mana is unchanged.** A disciple Draws as either colour (the Key's rider fires for the colour
drawn) and refuels the Channel of whichever colour is up; Prognosis, Accumulate, Void Sense, Expose and
Reaper's Harvest — the five deity cards that *refund* Investiture — become what they were always meant
to be, the trickle that keeps a one-point maintain running without a Draw.

### 1.7 R-108's Special target, restated from the measured 0 %

R-108 (a) restated the deity guide's ~15 – 20 % Special target and converted nothing, because the only
narrow conversion proposed changed no card and a broad one would widen the deity-vs-leyline agency gap
before leyline's agency work had landed. The Channel is that work, in design; so the target is
revisited here with the rules that moved leyline's Specials rather than by retyping to a number.

Three of the leyline conversion rules produce Specials, and each has deity consumers:

- **Rule 3 — a Reaction that is a modifier becomes a Special with "Once per round."** Five deity
  Reactions: Shatter Focus (a forced reroll, no test), Bonds of Community (temporary HP and advantage on
  a drop), Hexmark (a mark offered on a spring), Shoulder the Oath (a redirect), Combustion Chain (an
  automatic spread). None tests; all five are modifiers. Five Specials.
- **The armed-strike shape** — "*spend and make a weapon attack; on a hit, …*" — is Red's Volatile
  Strike, which the Red pass made a free Special with "Once per round." Withering Touch, Predatory
  Strike and Warlord's Advance are that shape exactly (each is an untimed self-status arm consumed on
  the next weapon hit in its `events`). Three Specials.
- **Rule 7 — a card that rides the payment** ("*when you channel or maintain …*", Guiding Signal's
  shape). No deity card is an obvious consumer at first pass; gates 2 – 4 may find one where a
  placement wants to happen every round without an Action.

That is **eight to ten Specials of ninety — about 10 %**, from 0 %. The published families run
30 – 41 % because their trees are mostly passives that modify one base action; the deity atlas is
built of two-Action installs by design (the guide says so, and Radiant's 10 % two-Action share is the
published high mark that deity's 23 % takes further). So the recommendation (D-7 (a)) is to **restate the
target to ~10 – 15 % Special**, reached by the three rules above and not chased: no standalone deity
Action is retyped to move the column. The alternative (D-7 (b)) is R-108's option (b) revived — one or
two standalone Actions per tree retyped at the gates — now legitimate on its own terms, since the
objection that held it was the agency work. The guide's action-type table is amended by this pass
either way.

### 1.9 The grouping for gates 2 – 4

The gate-1 answer groups the ten trees by what their spine *is*, which is also what the frames do to
them (§1.3), so each gate can carry one argument rather than three:

- **Gate 2 — the marks:** Chaos (Omens), Knowledge (Insight), Life (Diagnosis). One creature bears the
  spine; Blue's reading and Black's forsaking are the same shape, and the gate's question is how a mark
  and a frame on the same creature compose.
- **Gate 3 — the ground:** Fate (Ordained Ground, Snares), Destruction (Charges, dangerous terrain),
  Civilization (the Construct, Foundations), Death (Remains, the Bone Garden). The spine sits on the
  field; Green's home ground and White's line are the frames in play, and the gate's question is the
  three collisions §1.4 named plus what counts as "your terrain".
- **Gate 4 — the word:** Order (Edicts, Covenants), Power (Compelled, the Fury), Sovereignty (the
  die-step). The spine is a declaration on a creature; the gate's question is the arms (D-4) and the
  synthesis cards that gate on the amount (D-6).

### 1.10 Gate 1 — the menu

Every judgment call in §1, recommended default first. "Defaults on all except …" is enough.

**D-1. The gate a deity rider reads.** (a) **"While channelling [A] or [B]" — either of the tree's two
Channels, every rider live under both — recommended** (§1.2: six trees roll one colour on every test,
so colour-named riders would re-make the pure gate; the deity card was written to the god). (b) The
colour the card rolls or sizes with — each lane rides its own Channel; the other lane is dark.
(c) The tree's dominant colour, tree-wide. (d) A base action of the god's own, a third `power` on the
Channel's shape — listed to be refused (§1.2).

**D-2. The braid.** (a) **Rejected as a talent in every tree; the rider's "or" is the braid, and
M6 (c)'s allowance stands unused — recommended** (a braid card is a tax card in a nine-card tree; under
(D-1 (a)) it has nothing to unlock). (b) §1.6's braid passive as one of the nine in any tree whose
capstone is written to name both colours. (c) A rule-level braid in the base rule ("a deity disciple's
Channel of A counts as B for deity talents") — (a) in more words, and it widens §1.1, which M6 (c) said
a braid never does.

**D-3. The rider / release test.** (a) **The spine or either colour's domain → rider; outside all
three → release; the three leyline corollaries carry, and their six collisions with the spine clause are
menus at the tree gates — recommended** (§1.4; every spine is capped, which is why a generator can ride
where a bolt could not). (b) The union of the two colour domains alone, no spine clause — Kneel, Forge
Construct, Edict, Vital Diagnosis, Set Charge, Studied Mark, Exalt and Censure all fall outside their
colours' domains and stay costed; about thirty releases, and the tree's own resource is what the
disciple pays for. (c) Card by card at the gates with no stated test.

**D-4. Arms.** (a) **An arm the disciple wears — Crown of Thorns, Warlord's Fury, Necrotic Cascade,
Pack Share, The Pack, Concord — holds while the disciple channels either colour: "for the scene"
becomes "while you channel [A] or [B]"; zones, marks and pacts keep their printed duration —
recommended** (§1.5: the Channel is the upkeep, as it became Grasping Vines'; nearly free in the normal
case, and it gives Countercurrent and Unconscious a real prize). (b) Every rider keeps its printed
duration — the leyline rule verbatim; an arm is free once cast and outlasts the Channel.

**D-5. Capstones.** (a) **Releases: 3 Investiture (Raise Dead 4), once per scene, unchanged —
recommended** (the deity cost scale's own line; the rite is where a priced identity survives).
(b) Riders gated on the flood — "while channelling 3 or more [A] or [B]", free, once per scene — for
every tree. (c) Per tree at its gate: (a) by default, (b) where the capstone is the spine detonated.

**D-6. Named colours and the amount.** (a) **Default "A or B"; a card names one colour only where it
reads that colour's frame, and gates on "2 or more" only where its 2-Investiture price marks the heavier
play — each a per-card menu at its gate — recommended.** (b) No deity card ever names a colour or an
amount.

**D-7. R-108's Special target.** (a) **Restated to ~10 – 15 %, reached by rule 3 (five modifier
Reactions), the armed-strike shape (Withering Touch, Predatory Strike, Warlord's Advance) and rule 7
where a consumer appears; no standalone Action retyped to move the column; the guide's table amended —
recommended** (§1.7). (b) Keep 15 – 20 % and retype one or two standalone Actions per tree at the gates
(R-108 (b), now legitimate: the objection was leyline's agency work, and the Channel is it). (c) Keep
15 – 20 % as an aspiration and let the rules land where they land.

**D-8. Countercurrent and deity releases (BL-7's hand-off).** (a) **Counterable: the card's trigger
reads "spends Investiture on a talent or a Channel" — the word *leyline* dropped, one word on Blue's
card in item 198's Blue leg — recommended** (§1.6 item 3; the phrase is exact because only leyline and
deity talents spend Investiture). (b) Narrow deliberately: deity releases are the god's and Blue cannot
answer them; the card says so. (c) Widen to "an Investiture-costing talent or a Channel" — the same
scope as (a) in a longer phrase.

**D-9. The grouping for gates 2 – 4.** (a) **The marks (Chaos, Knowledge, Life) / the ground (Fate,
Destruction, Civilization, Death) / the word (Order, Power, Sovereignty) — recommended** (§1.9: one
argument per gate). (b) By colour pair — the three Black trees, the three Green trees, the rest.
(c) One tree per gate, ten gates.
